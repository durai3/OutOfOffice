# Terms of Service

**Effective 23 September 2026. Version 2026-09-23.1.**

you rely on DELEG8 for anything that matters commercially.

## 1. What DELEG8 does

DELEG8 sends an automated participant ("the delegate") into a video meeting you
point it at. In that meeting the delegate:

- joins under a name you configure and identifies itself as an AI when asked;
- transcribes what it hears in order to decide whether and how to respond;
- speaks synthesised audio into the call on your behalf, within the limits of
  the Representation Policy you configure;
- asks you for approval, over your notification channel, before saying anything
  your policy marks as requiring it;
- writes you a summary after the meeting ends.

delegate says. Keep your password to yourself; tell us immediately if you think
someone else has it.

## 3. Your responsibility for the meetings you join

This is the important clause. **You decide which meetings the delegate joins,
and you are solely responsible for having the right to send it there.**

Specifically, you confirm that for every meeting you dispatch a delegate to:

- you are an invited participant, or you have the organiser's permission to
  send a delegate in your place;
- you have the legal right, in every jurisdiction where a participant is
  located, to have that meeting transcribed by an automated participant; and
- you have obtained any consent that the law where the participants are
  located requires. Many jurisdictions require every party to a call to
  consent before it may be recorded or transcribed. DELEG8 provides tooling to
  announce the delegate and to collect spoken consent (see section 4), but the
  legal obligation is yours, not ours.

You must not use DELEG8 to join a meeting covertly, to record someone who has
objected, to impersonate a specific human being in a way intended to deceive,
or for any unlawful purpose.

## 4. Consent tooling

Every delegate announces itself on joining and identifies itself as an AI when
asked. You may additionally configure "all-party consent" mode, in which the
delegate asks the room for agreement and retains no transcript, memory, summary
or action items unless it receives it. We record what was announced, what was
said in reply, and the outcome, and you can export that record.

These are tools to help you comply. They are not a legal opinion that your use
is compliant.
